By installing or using Vest Copier you agree to the following. The panel asks you to accept these terms before
it can be used.

## It trades for you

Vest Copier places **real orders on live trading accounts**, automatically, using your logged-in Vest session.
Once armed, an order you place on your master account is sent to every follower account you selected.

## Things can go wrong

Copies can be late, fail, fill at a different price or size, or be missed entirely. This can happen, for
example, when Vest changes its website or API, rejects or delays an order, rate-limits requests, or when your
browser, network or computer has a problem. One bad trade on the master hits every linked account at once.
Watch your accounts while the copier runs, and size so that a loss on every account at the same time is
survivable.
